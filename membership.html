MEMBERSHIP PAYS FOR ITSELF

Membership is $20 a year — half of that is the state association fee, and the other $10 stays here and buys groomer fuel. New Hampshire gives club members $30 off the registration of every sled they own, so register even one sled and you are already ahead.

Two sleds in the household? The discount is $60 against the same $20 membership — you finish $40 ahead.

A family membership extends the discount to your partner and any children under 18 living at the same address.

How to join — about 5 minutes

  1. Sign up online through the NH Snowmobile Association and pick Great Bay Sno-Rollers as your club.
  2. Your NHSA membership voucher arrives by email immediately — you can show it right from your phone.
  3. Bring the voucher when you register your sled. You must show the voucher to get the discount; knowing your member number is not enough.
Join Online Now

NH snowmobile registrations run May 1 through April 30, so joining before you register is what unlocks the discount for the whole season. Questions? Email greatbaysnorollers@gmail.com.

❓ Common Questions

Do I have to live in Newmarket to join?
No. Anyone can join Great Bay Sno-Rollers, and plenty of our members ride all over the state.

Can I join more than one club?
Yes, and a lot of riders do. You only need one voucher for the registration discount, but joining a second club supports their trails too.

What if I don't own a sled yet?
Join anyway. Dues support grooming, and you're welcome on group rides and at meetings either way.

I already registered my sled this year. Did I miss out?
The discount applies at the time you register, so it can't be applied afterwards. Join now and it'll be waiting when you renew — NH registrations run May 1 through April 30.

Where does my money actually go?
$10 goes to the NH Snowmobile Association. The other $10 stays with the club and goes toward grooming fuel, equipment repair, signage, and bridge materials. Nobody here takes a salary. See the groomers and equipment your dues keep running.
