January Meeting - January 6th

📅 January 6, 2026

Great Bay Sno-Rollers January Meeting

I hope this message finds you well and you had a great New Year! Our monthly January meeting is coming up, and I'd like to invite you all to join us.

This will be a combined meeting with the members of the Lee Sno-Travelers.

Meeting Details:

  • Date: Tuesday, January 6th
  • Time: 6:00 PM
  • Location: My shop - 31 Tuckers Way, Newmarket, NH 03857
  • ⚠️ The parking area is VERY ICY, please use caution

Agenda Items: We have several important topics to discuss as we prepare for the season ahead:

  • Trail work updates and upcoming projects
  • Signage installation and maintenance
  • Club clothing orders and availability
  • Grooming schedule and coordination
  • Groomer maintenance and equipment status
  • Potential expansion: Taking over management of other southern NH clubs

Feel free to bring any questions or concerns you'd like to discuss. Looking forward to seeing everyone there!

PS - Bring a camp chair and the shop/meeting is beer friendly.
