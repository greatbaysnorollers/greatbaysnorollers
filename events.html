📆 Event Calendar

🎉 Types of Events

🏔️ Club Rides

Organized group rides through our trail system. All skill levels welcome. Check trail conditions before each ride.

📋 Monthly Meetings

Regular club meetings to discuss trail maintenance, upcoming events, and club business. Members encouraged to attend.

🛠️ Trail Work Days

Volunteer opportunities to help maintain and improve our trail network. Bring tools and be ready to work!

💰 Fundraisers

Special events to raise funds for trail grooming equipment, maintenance, and club operations.

🎊 Social Events

Family-friendly gatherings, dinners, and celebrations to build community among club members.

🏆 Competitions

Organized races, poker runs, and other competitive events for the snowmobile community.

💡 Event Tips

  • RSVP when requested - Helps organizers plan for food, parking, and logistics
  • Check weather and trail conditions - Outdoor events may be rescheduled
  • Bring a friend - New members are always welcome at our events
  • Follow our Facebook page - Get real-time updates and last-minute changes
