January Meeting - January 6th

📅 January 6, 2026

Great Bay Sno-Rollers January Meeting

I hope this message finds you well and you had a great New Year! Our monthly January meeting is coming up, and I'd like to invite you all to join us.

This will be a combined meeting with the members of the Lee Sno-Travelers.

Meeting Details:

  • Date: Tuesday, January 6th
  • Time: 6:00 PM
  • Location: My shop - 31 Tuckers Way, Newmarket, NH 03857
  • ⚠️ The parking area is VERY ICY, please use caution

Agenda Items: We have several important topics to discuss as we prepare for the season ahead:

  • Trail work updates and upcoming projects
  • Signage installation and maintenance
  • Club clothing orders and availability
  • Grooming schedule and coordination
  • Groomer maintenance and equipment status
  • Potential expansion: Taking over management of other southern NH clubs

Feel free to bring any questions or concerns you'd like to discuss. Looking forward to seeing everyone there!

PS - Bring a camp chair and the shop/meeting is beer friendly.

🛠️ Trail Grooming & Maintenance

Keeping our trails in top condition

Trail Grooming Schedule

Our grooming crews work throughout the season to maintain trail quality. Grooming typically occurs:

  • Main trails: 2-3 times per week during peak season
  • Secondary trails: Weekly or as needed
  • Best riding: 24-48 hours after grooming

Check our Facebook page for real-time grooming updates and schedules.

Volunteer Trail Work

Help us maintain our trail system! Volunteer opportunities include:

  • Spring/Fall trail clearing and brush cutting
  • Bridge and signage maintenance
  • Trail marking and mapping
  • Equipment maintenance and support

Want to help? Contact us at greatbaysnorollers@gmail.com or check our Events Calendar for upcoming trail work days.

Support Our Grooming

Trail grooming equipment and fuel are expensive. Your support helps keep trails in excellent condition:

  • Become a member - Dues support grooming operations
  • Make a donation - Every dollar helps maintain equipment
  • Volunteer time - Reduce costs by helping with maintenance
  • Spread the word - More members = better trails
