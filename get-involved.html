GET INVOLVED

Trail work, grooming, and the people who keep it running

← Back to Home

The trails don't groom themselves. Everything Great Bay Sno-Rollers does is volunteer work — here's how to jump in, whatever amount of time you've got.

🔨 What Needs Doing

You do not need special skills or your own equipment. Show up and we'll find you a job.

  • Fall brushing and clearing — cutting back growth before the snow flies
  • Bridge and signage work — building, repairing, and hanging trail markers
  • Storm cleanup — clearing blowdowns after a big blow
  • Groomer maintenance — wrenching, welding, and keeping the drag together
  • Grooming shifts — training provided; we'll ride with you first
  • Landowner relations — saying thank you, and meaning it

Interested? Email greatbaysnorollers@gmail.com or just come to a monthly meeting.
